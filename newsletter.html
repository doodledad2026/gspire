The Goldenspire Letter

Practical help for small businesses, every week

AI, automation and business systems explained in plain English, for owners anywhere who are great at their work and short on time. One useful idea a week, plus a free resource library the day you join.

I spend my days building AI systems for global companies. The tools that make those companies faster are now cheap, or free, for a five-person business. But nobody explains them in plain English, and nobody does the math on whether they're worth it for your business. That's what this letter does.

What it covers

  • Winning more customers: faster follow-up, missed calls, reviews, websites that turn visitors into inquiries.
  • Getting paid and staying organized: invoicing, scheduling, paperwork and the admin that eats your evenings.
  • Using AI the right way: prompts you can copy, what's worth automating, and what's hype.
  • Free tools that are actually worth it: what to use, what to skip, and the limits nobody mentions.
  • The real math: what a problem costs you per year, and when fixing it pays for itself.
  • Building a business that runs without you: simple systems so it doesn't all live in your head.
