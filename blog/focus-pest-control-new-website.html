Focus Pest Control: A New Website Built to Bring In Local Calls

A Dillsburg pest control company, a new website, and one goal: when a neighbor searches for help, they find Focus and call.

Share

Focus Pest Control is a local pest control company based here in Dillsburg, treating mice, wasps and other insects for homes across Mechanicsburg, Dillsburg, Boiling Springs, York Springs, Wellsville, Lake Meade, White Rock Acres and nearby areas.

Pest problems are urgent. Someone who finds wasps by the back door or hears mice in the walls searches, picks a company and calls, often within minutes. Focus asked us to build a new website around that moment: be easy to find, be easy to understand, and be easy to call.

What we built

  • A new website, start to finish. A fresh design and layout that leads with what Focus treats and where it works, instead of making visitors dig.
  • Navigation that matches how customers think. Clear pages for services, the tri-annual service plan, savings, what makes Focus different, and contact, all one tap from the menu.
  • Contact at every turn. Call Now and Email Us buttons up front, with the phone number and email in plain view, so a worried homeowner can reach Focus without hunting for it.
  • Services people recognize. Mice, wasps and all other insects, each with a short, plain description of how Focus handles it.
  • Local search, done properly. Page titles, descriptions and content written around pest control in Dillsburg and the specific towns Focus serves, so the site can show up when neighbors search for help nearby.

Why it matters for a local service business

For a business like Focus, the website is often the first conversation. Most people compare two or three companies and call whoever looks credible and answers first. A site that is clear about the problem, the service area and how to get in touch does a lot of that selling before the phone ever rings.

It also pairs naturally with the rest of what we build. A missed-call text-back catches the callers who reach voicemail, and a review request after every job keeps Focus's Google profile fresh for the next search.
