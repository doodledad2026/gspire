An Esthetician Studio in Mechanicsburg: A Website Built to Book Appointments

A skincare studio in Mechanicsburg, a website built from scratch, and one goal: make it easy for a new client to trust the studio and book.

Share

This client is a licensed esthetician and makeup artist with her own skincare studio in Mechanicsburg, offering facials, chemical peels, makeup, waxing, advanced treatments and holistic services.

In a business like hers, almost every new client starts the same way: a friend recommends the studio, or someone searches for a facial nearby, and then they check the website before deciding whether to book. We built the whole site around that moment.

What we built

  • A complete website, from scratch. Home, about, services, featured products, testimonials and contact pages, designed to feel calm and personal, like the studio itself.
  • Booking on every page. A Book Now button in the header of every page connects straight to her online booking, so a visitor can go from reading about a treatment to choosing a time in a couple of taps.
  • Gift cards, built in. A Gift Cards button sits next to Book Now and links to online gift card ordering, an easy extra sale around birthdays and holidays.
  • A services menu people can scan. Treatments are organized into clear groups: facials, chemical peels, makeup, advanced treatments, waxing, holistic services and add-ons.
  • Reviews front and center. Her Google reviews appear on the homepage, there's a dedicated testimonials page, and a link makes it easy for happy clients to leave a new review.
  • Contact without confusion. Phone, email and an embedded map on the contact page, plus clear arrival instructions for the building's entry system, so first-time clients aren't left standing at the door.
  • The details on every page. Opening hours, the studio address and links to Facebook, Instagram and TikTok sit in the footer of every page.
  • Search-ready from day one. An SEO tool is set up so each page has its own title and description, plus a photo gallery of the studio and a built-in translate option for visitors.

Why it matters for a service business

For a studio that runs on appointments, the website does two jobs: it earns trust, and it takes the booking. Reviews, real photos of the space and a clear menu of treatments answer a new client's questions before they ask them. Booking and gift cards one tap away from every page turn that trust into an appointment, even when the studio is busy with a client and can't answer the phone.

It also pairs naturally with the rest of what we build, like automatic appointment reminders that cut no-shows and a review request after every visit.
