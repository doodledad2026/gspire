Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this covers

Goldenspire Group ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. This policy covers goldenspiregroup.com and doesn't apply to systems we build for individual clients, which are governed by that client's own privacy practices and any service agreement in place.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly: when you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, business name, email address, and whatever details you share about your business needs. We use this solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the work discussed.

Automatically collected information: like most websites, basic technical information (browser type, general location by IP, pages visited) may be collected through standard web hosting and analytics tools to understand how the site is used and to keep it running reliably.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, provide consultations, and deliver services if you engage us. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

Third-party services

This site may use third-party services for hosting, form submissions, and basic analytics. These providers may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of delivering their service, under their own privacy policies. We choose providers with reasonable privacy practices, but we encourage you to review their policies if you have specific concerns.

Data retention and security

We retain contact form submissions and client information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry or fulfill a service agreement. We take reasonable steps to protect information you share with us, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing info@goldenspiregroup.com. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@goldenspiregroup.com, or by phone to the number listed on our Contact page.
