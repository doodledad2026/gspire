Questions About These Services

What people ask before picking a service.

Do I need all of these services, or can I start with one?

Start with one. Most engagements begin with whatever's costing you the most time or leads right now, usually intake, scheduling, or CRM, and expand from there once the first system is proven out.

Can you integrate with the software we already use?

In almost every case, yes. We've connected automation to platforms like QuickBooks, Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Clio, and dozens of others. If something needs an upgrade to do the job right, we'll tell you before any work starts.

What if the service I need isn't listed here?

This list covers what comes up most often, not everything we can build. If your business has a repetitive process that isn't on this page, reach out and describe it: there's a good chance it's automatable.

Who owns the system once it's built?

You do. Every build runs on accounts in your name, not ours. If you ever stop working with Goldenspire, the system keeps running exactly as it did the day before.

Build & Handover or Build & Monitor: which one do I need?

If your team is comfortable owning the system day to day, Build & Handover is usually enough. If you'd rather not think about it, Build & Monitor adds ongoing monitoring and updates for a flat monthly fee. See the full comparison for details.

Are these custom builds or templates?

Custom. Off-the-shelf tools get you generic automation that half-fits your workflow. Every Goldenspire system is built around how your specific business operates, which is also why it actually gets used six months later.
