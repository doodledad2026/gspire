Never Miss a Lead: Automated Customer Intake & Follow-Up

Every call, text, and web inquiry gets a reply the moment it lands, day or night, so no lead sits waiting for someone to notice it.

The Problem

What's actually costing time and revenue

  • A call comes in after hours or during a busy stretch, goes to voicemail, and the caller moves to the next name on the list
  • A web form or text sits unanswered for hours because no one owns checking it consistently
  • Following up on older leads gets pushed aside for whatever feels urgent today, so warm leads quietly go cold
The Approach

How we close the gap

Every inbound channel — phone, text, web form, chat — gets watched and answered immediately. New inquiries are captured, qualified against basic criteria, and either answered directly or routed to the right person with the context already attached, so nothing depends on someone happening to be free at that exact moment.

What Gets Built

The system, piece by piece

  • Instant reply to calls, texts, and web inquiries outside business hours or during busy periods
  • Automatic lead qualification and routing to the right person or team
  • Structured follow-up sequences for leads that don't convert on the first touch
  • A record of every inquiry and its outcome, so nothing falls through the cracks
Typical Impact

Typically means every inbound lead gets a response within minutes instead of hours, and fewer of them go to whichever competitor answered first.
