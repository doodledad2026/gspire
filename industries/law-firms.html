Competitive Intelligence

Know what other firms in your market are doing, without checking manually

Most firms find out about a competitor's move secondhand, and by then it's already cost them a case. We set up automated monitoring on the signals that actually predict a competitor's next move: new attorney hires and departures (often visible on their site or LinkedIn before it's announced), freshly published practice-area pages, changes to advertised fees or intake process, review volume and rating shifts on Google and Avvo, and, where public, filing volume by practice area in your local courts. A scheduled process checks these sources on its own and flags what actually changed, rather than someone manually re-checking a handful of competitor websites every few months and missing everything in between.

That feed rolls into a monitoring dashboard built for your firm: one screen showing which competitors are moving into which practice areas, how their review counts and ratings are trending, and where their marketing is shifting, updated automatically instead of compiled by hand before a partner meeting.

What Changes

What gets built

  • Client intake forms that route to the right attorney by practice area
  • Document summarization for faster case prep
  • Automated appointment confirmation and reminder systems
  • Case status updates that keep clients informed without extra calls
  • A competitive intelligence dashboard tracking rival firms' websites, review trends, and practice-area moves in one place
  • Automated alerts when a competing firm launches a new practice area, changes pricing or intake process, or sees a spike in reviews or ad activity
