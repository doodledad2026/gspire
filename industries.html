By Industry

What we actually build, by business type.

Every business is different, but these are the specific systems that show up most often in each industry.

Real Estate

Agents live or die by response speed and never having a lead go cold.

  • Automated lead follow-up the moment an inquiry comes in from a listing site or your own website
  • AI-drafted listing descriptions from photos and basic specs
  • Showing-schedule automation synced to your calendar
  • Buyer/seller intake forms that route straight into your CRM or a simple tracking sheet

HVAC & Home Services

Missed calls and slow quote follow-up send jobs straight to a competitor.

  • 24/7 call and text triage, urgent vs. routine, routed appropriately
  • Automated appointment booking and dispatch scheduling
  • Quote follow-up sequences so estimates don't go cold
  • Post-job review requests sent automatically

Insurance Agencies

Renewal reminders and policy FAQs eat staff time that should go to actual client relationships.

  • Policy FAQ assistant trained on your actual coverage documents
  • Automated renewal and appointment reminders
  • Claims intake documentation and status updates
  • Compliance-aware client communication, built with your review before anything goes live

Credit Unions & Financial Services

Member onboarding and appointment scheduling often run on manual back-and-forth.

  • Member onboarding and document collection automation
  • Appointment scheduling for loan officers and advisors
  • Renewal and maturity-date reminder sequences
  • Secure document intake, built to your compliance requirements

Law Firms

An unanswered inquiry is a client who hired someone else. The intake moment matters most.

  • Client intake forms that route to the right attorney by practice area
  • Document summarization for faster case prep
  • Automated appointment confirmation and reminder systems
  • Case status updates that keep clients informed without extra calls

Business Brokers

Sorting serious buyers from tire-kickers manually costs real deal time.

  • Buyer inquiry qualification before it reaches your inbox
  • AI-drafted listing summaries and teasers
  • Deal document summarization
  • Automated status updates across a long, multi-party close

Auto Services

Customers who don't hear back quickly call the next shop on the list.

  • Automated, itemized text updates for every repair stage
  • Appointment scheduling and dispatch
  • Post-service review requests
  • After-hours and overnight call triage for emergency shops

Retail & Local Shops

Casual visitors become standing customers when ordering and rebooking are effortless.

  • Online ordering or call-ahead systems for specialty items
  • Loyalty and rebooking reminders
  • Automated review requests after purchase or visit
  • Simple inventory-based customer notifications

Architecture & Design

Long project timelines mean a lot of manual status-checking between milestones.

  • Automated client updates between meetings on long projects
  • Permit and code-requirement tracking assistance
  • Project inquiry intake and initial scoping forms
  • Milestone and check-in automation for multi-year client relationships

Manufacturing & Distribution

Order and quote intake often still runs through phone calls and email threads.

  • Automated quote-request and order-tracking systems
  • Dealer or distributor inquiry routing
  • Parts and order status automation
  • Production scheduling reminder systems

Professional Services (General)

Almost every office-based business shares the same repetitive admin bottlenecks.

  • Client intake and document automation
  • Meeting scheduling and confirmation sequences
  • Automated follow-up so nothing falls through the cracks
  • Custom dashboards pulling from your existing data sources
How We Build It

Deep experience across a wide array of platforms, connected to what you already run.

Most businesses already have some tools in place: QuickBooks, Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Clio, Mailchimp, Square, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or something similar. We don't rip that out and replace it.

We've built automation across dozens of platforms and AI systems, so we connect to what you've already invested in instead of letting it go to waste. And if a piece of your stack needs an upgrade to get the job done right, that breadth of experience means we know exactly what to recommend and how to set it up ourselves. Either way, everything runs on accounts you own, not ours, so you're never locked into needing us to keep it running.
