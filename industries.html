By Industry

What we actually build, by business type.

Every business is different, but these are the problems, approaches, and typical outcomes that show up most often in each industry. Illustrative scenarios based on the kind of work described, not claims about a specific past client.

Real Estate

The Problems
  • A lead texts at 9pm about a listing and doesn't hear back until the next afternoon, by then they've toured three other houses
  • Buyers go quiet after the third showing because nobody followed up at the right moment
  • Weekend inquiries pile up until Monday, when half of them have already found another agent
The Approach

Intercept every new inquiry the moment it lands, qualify it, and put follow-up in motion before the agent even sees it.

What Changes
  • Automated lead follow-up the moment an inquiry comes in from a listing site or your own website
  • AI-drafted listing descriptions from photos and basic specs
  • Showing-schedule automation synced to your calendar
  • Buyer/seller intake forms that route straight into your CRM or a simple tracking sheet
Typical Impact

Typically the difference between a lead who books a showing and one who goes cold by the next morning.

HVAC & Home Services

The Problems
  • A no-heat call at 10pm goes to voicemail, and the homeowner calls the next name on the list
  • A quote gets emailed and never followed up on, so it just sits there
  • Techs spend half their morning confirming appointments instead of on the truck
The Approach

Round-the-clock call and text triage paired with automated scheduling, so no job sits waiting on a callback.

What Changes
  • 24/7 call and text triage, urgent vs. routine, routed appropriately
  • Automated appointment booking and dispatch scheduling
  • Quote follow-up sequences so estimates don't go cold
  • Post-job review requests sent automatically
Typical Impact

Typically recovers after-hours calls that would otherwise go to whichever shop answers first.

An assistant trained on your actual coverage documents handles routine questions and renewal nudges, reviewed by you before anything goes live.

What Changes
  • Policy FAQ assistant trained on your actual coverage documents

  • Compliance-aware client communication, built with your review before anything goes live
Typical Impact

Typically frees several hours a week back into actual client conversations instead of repetitive admin.

Typically means the first client meeting starts already informed, not spent gathering basics.

How We Build It

Deep experience across a wide array of platforms, connected to what you already run.

Most businesses already have some tools in place: QuickBooks, Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Clio, Mailchimp, Square, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or something similar. We don't rip that out and replace it.

We've built automation across dozens of platforms and AI systems, so we connect to what you've already invested in instead of letting it go to waste. And if a piece of your stack needs an upgrade to get the job done right, that breadth of experience means we know exactly what to recommend and how to set it up ourselves. Either way, everything runs on accounts you own, not ours, so you're never locked into needing us to keep it running.
