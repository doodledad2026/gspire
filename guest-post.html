Content Guidelines

We Accept Posts That:

  • Are 1,000–2,500 words (shorter is fine for tactical posts)
  • Solve a real business problem (not theoretical or generic)
  • Include specific examples, numbers, or frameworks
  • Help owners make better decisions about automation, hiring, or systems
  • Are original and not published elsewhere (except your own blog)

We Don't Accept:

  • Thinly veiled product pitches or sales pages
  • Generic "10 tips for X" without real substance
  • Content that's already live on other sites
  • Posts that redirect readers away from the core topic

How to Submit

  1. Send us a pitch first. Email info@goldenspiregroup.com with:
    • Your name & credentials
    • Post title or topic
    • A 1-2 sentence summary
    • Link to your website or previous published work
  2. We'll review and get back in 3-5 days. If we like the pitch, we'll confirm and discuss any specific angle or audience focus.
  3. Write and submit. Send us the completed post (Google Doc, Word, or plain text). We'll do a light edit for clarity and voice.
  4. We publish and promote. Your post goes live on our blog with an author bio & backlink to your site. We'll share it via email, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

Author Bio

Each post includes an author bio. Keep it to 50-75 words. Include:

  • Who you are & what you do
  • Your website or LinkedIn profile
  • One sentence about your expertise
