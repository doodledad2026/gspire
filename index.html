Common questions

What business owners ask before reaching out.

Straight answers, no sales spin. Anything else, just ask.

What does this cost?
Our starter packages start at $250 to $500 one-time, and larger builds are scoped after a free consultation. Every engagement is a flat project fee, not billed hourly, so you know the full cost before any work starts. Most projects pay for themselves within 2 to 3 months from time saved or leads recovered.
How long does a build take?
Most single-workflow automations (intake, scheduling, reviews) go live in 1 to 2 weeks. Larger, multi-step systems take longer, and you'll know the timeline before any work starts.
What if it doesn't fit how we work?
Every build starts with how your team actually operates, not a generic template. And every system ships with a 30-day warranty: bugs or gaps against the original scope get fixed at no charge.
Do we need special software or technical skills?
No. We connect to what you already use, drawing on hands-on experience across a wide range of business platforms and AI systems, or set up what's missing. You get a plain-English explanation of what the system does, not a technical manual to decipher.
Are we locked into a contract?
No. Everything runs on accounts you own, and support after delivery is month-to-month. If Goldenspire isn't earning its keep, you can walk away with a system that still works.
Do you reward referrals?
Yes. For every business you refer that becomes a client, you choose 30 more days of free fixes or $50 off your next project. Referral rewards stack.
Why not just hire a big agency or DIY it?
Agencies bring overhead and long contracts. DIY tools take real time to configure and maintain yourself. Goldenspire gives you dedicated, full-time AI expertise without the agency markup, with a real person who answers the phone.
Do you only work with small, local businesses?
No. I'm based in South Central Pennsylvania and love meeting local clients face-to-face, but the systems I build run the same whether you're five minutes away or five states away. The same standard applies to every engagement, regardless of size or location.
Do you have case studies or past client results?
Yes, client work is currently underway.
