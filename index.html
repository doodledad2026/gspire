FAQs

Questions owners ask first.

Anything else, just ask.

What does this cost?
Our starter packages start at $250 to $500 one-time, and larger builds are scoped after a free consultation. Every engagement is a flat project fee, not billed hourly, so you know the full cost before any work starts. Most projects pay for themselves within 2 to 3 months from time saved or leads recovered.
How long does a build take?
Most single-workflow automations (intake, scheduling, reviews) go live in 1 to 2 weeks. Larger, multi-step systems take longer, and you'll know the timeline before any work starts.
Do we need special software or technical skills?
No. We connect to what you already use, or set up what's missing. You get a plain-English explanation of what the system does, not a technical manual.
Are we locked into a contract?
No. Everything runs on accounts you own, and support after delivery is month-to-month. If Goldenspire isn't earning its keep, you can walk away with a system that still works.
What happens after the build?
Your choice. With Build & Handover, you get a complete, documented system and your team runs it. With Build & Monitor, we watch it for a monthly fee, catch issues early and update it as your business changes. Either way, every build has a 30-day warranty.
Do you only work with local businesses?
No. We're based in South Central Pennsylvania and love meeting local clients face-to-face, but the systems run the same whether you're five minutes away or five states away. See who we serve.
Do you reward referrals?
Yes. For every business you refer that becomes a client, you choose 30 more days of free fixes or $50 off your next project. Referral rewards stack.
Do you have case studies?
Yes. For confidentiality we don't publish client names, logos or specific numbers, but you can read the apparel shop story and see what these systems look like day to day. Ask for a live, anonymized walkthrough when you reach out.
